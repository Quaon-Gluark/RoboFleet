About RoboFleet

RoboFleet is a student-led project focused on developing autonomous robotic systems designed for real-world impact. The project explores how drones and ground-based robots can work together to solve complex challenges in environments where human access is limited or dangerous.

At its core, RoboFleet combines programming, mechanical design, and electronics to create systems that are both intelligent and practical. Rather than focusing purely on theory, the project emphasises building, testing, and refining working prototypes.

The vision behind RoboFleet is to make robotics more accessible and adaptable. By focusing on modular design and scalable solutions, the project aims to demonstrate how advanced technology can be applied in a cost-effective and realistic way.

This project also represents a learning journey-developing skills in problem-solving, engineering, and collaboration. It highlights how modern robotics requires an interdisciplinary approach, bringing together multiple areas of knowledge to create meaningful solutions.
